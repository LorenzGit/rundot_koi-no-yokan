## Presentation notes

- Keep Mizuki on the left and the current scene partner on the right.
- Swap supporting characters rather than crowding the stage.
- Use the full location image as the background. Do not use location thumbnails as backgrounds.
- The supplied sprites are transparent cutouts. Do not add rectangular frames behind them.
- The optional `in-love.png` files are visual alternates, not a supported Ink emotion name.
