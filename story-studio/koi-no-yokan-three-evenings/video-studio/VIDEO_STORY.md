# Koi no Yokan: Three Evenings — video treatment

## Format

- Three vertical anime-romance shorts.
- About 76, 97, and 102 seconds.
- Sole romance: Rin (woman) and Haruto (man).
- Tone: quiet, hopeful, gently funny, emotionally direct.
- Palette: sakura pink, sea blue, and warm restaurant gold.

Rin and Haruto are the leads. Kaede is Rin's blunt confidante; Kaito runs the
community film festival and repeatedly pairs Rin with Haruto. Kaede and Kaito
have no romance with each other or with either lead. No other romantic
relationship appears in the video world.

Every scene includes dialogue. The ellipses, em dashes, sentence breaks, and
occasional doubled punctuation are intentional TTS pacing marks. Do not
normalize or remove them when editing in Video Studio.

## Premise

Meticulous festival organizer Rin cannot stand careless, joking Haruto after he
arrives late and spills coffee across three weeks of her work. Kaito needs one
more volunteer, and Kaede refuses to let Rin dismiss him without a second
chance. Across three festival evenings, Haruto's quiet reliability dismantles
Rin's first impression. Her anger becomes respect, anticipation, and finally a
love she must say aloud before Haruto walks away.

## Episode 1 — The Worst First Impression

At Sakura Plaza, Rin prepares the opening night of Kaito's community film
festival with Kaede. Haruto arrives late, trips on a cable, and spills coffee
over Rin's color-coded schedule. Rin is furious. Kaito reveals that Haruto is
their missing volunteer, and Kaede tells Rin to keep him close enough to prove
himself. When rain threatens the festival, Haruto protects Rin's posters and
the film reel instead of himself. She still does not like him—but grants him one
shift to change her mind.

## Episode 2 — When the Tide Turns

At Beach Terrace, Kaito assigns Rin and Haruto to deliver the ticket box while
Kaede blocks Rin's escape. Wind scatters the tickets, and the pair save them by
working together. Haruto admits that he jokes when nervous; Rin admits that
carelessness makes her feel dismissed. Kaede catches Rin smiling, and Kaito
reveals that Haruto secretly repaired every coffee-damaged page of her schedule.
At sunset, Rin stays when their shoulders touch and concedes that she does not
hate him “as much.”

## Episode 3 — Before the Lights Go Out

At La Dolce Vita, Kaede confronts Rin for checking the door every few seconds.
Across the restaurant, Kaito tells Haruto to stop hiding his feelings behind
jokes. Haruto gives Rin a bouquet and a framed piece of the stained schedule,
then says he plans to leave so she can enjoy a peaceful ending. Rin gets angry
again—this time because he never asked what she wanted. She admits that between
being furious and waiting for him to arrive, she fell in love. Haruto tells her
he loves her clearly, without a joke. Kaede and Kaito celebrate by giving them
two tickets for their first official date.

## Ending image

Rin says she loves Haruto, warns him never to carry her coffee, and pulls him
into a quick kiss while Kaede cheers and Kaito lights the restaurant around
them.
