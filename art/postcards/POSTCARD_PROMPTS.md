# Selected vacation postcard prompts

This file records the selected production prompts and the rejected Rin/Haruto
V1 prompts for the eight-character vacation postcard collection. Kaede's exact
approved prompt is retained in `README.md` under **Kaede swimsuit revision**.
All references are project-owned artwork.

## Mizuki — granite cove mural

> Use case: stylized-concept
>
> Asset type: collectible vacation postcard illustration for KOI NO YOKAN
>
> Input images: Image 1 is the sole identity/anatomy reference for
> Mizuki—preserve her recognizable adult face, blue-gray eyes, extremely long
> flowing dark teal hair, slim feminine proportions, skin tone, and
> multicolored painter's nails. Image 2 is ONLY the approved postcard finish,
> cream border, cel-art quality, and extreme motion reference; do not copy
> Kaede's face, body, hair, swimsuit, location, or pose.
>
> Primary request: Generate a new polished landscape vacation postcard of
> Mizuki in a cute tropical swimsuit, doing an artist-specific action in a
> highly dynamic full-body pose.
>
> Scene/backdrop: a distinctive sunrise tropical cove filled with enormous
> smooth silver-and-rose granite boulders, pale sand, glass-clear ankle-deep
> water, sea grapes, turquoise horizon, and small colorful reef fish. Exactly
> one character.
>
> Wardrobe: replace all original clothing with a tasteful ivory one-piece
> swimsuit featuring a teal watercolor-gradient sash, one softly ruffled
> shoulder strap, secure coverage, and a small sea-glass hair clip. Bare feet.
> No cardigan or dress.
>
> Subject/action: Freeze Mizuki in a spectacular airborne skipping leap across
> a shallow tide pool while painting a vacation mural. Her torso twists sharply
> in three-quarter profile; one arm whips a long paintbrush overhead, releasing
> a beautiful curved ribbon of turquoise and coral paint droplets through the
> air; the other hand holds a compact painter's palette securely against her
> ribs. One knee drives high and forward, the opposite leg stretches far
> backward with pointed toes, creating a large asymmetric split silhouette. Her
> immense teal hair streams in a dramatic S-curve opposite the jump. She gives
> a delighted, slightly surprised laugh and both eyes focus ahead on a blank
> sun-bleached driftwood mural panel near her landing point—not at the viewer.
> Paint must remain a plausible controlled splash, not obscure her body.
>
> Style/medium: closely preserve Image 1's classic 1990s Japanese television-
> anime character design; crisp thin dark ink, expressive face, correct anatomy,
> flat two-tone cel shading, saturated tropical palette, hand-painted
> animation-background feeling. Match Image 2's polished postcard finish and
> motion intensity. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not glossy modern rendering.
>
> Composition/framing: wide 3:2 landscape postcard; complete figure visible
> from hair tips to toes with generous safety margin. Strong lower-left to
> upper-right flight path; paint arc and streaming hair reinforce the movement.
> Use a clean cream printed-postcard border with softly rounded corners,
> identical in spirit to Image 2, with no writing.
>
> Lighting/mood: pearly sunrise, playful, creative, dreamy, breezy.
>
> Constraints: preserve Mizuki's identity and hair color/length; exactly one
> adult character; new swimsuit; anatomically correct hands and feet; palette
> and brush held coherently; face and eyes aligned with travel direction;
> distinctive granite-cove location; full body unobstructed; finished
> game-quality illustration.
>
> Avoid: text, letters, logos, stamps, captions, watermark, extra people,
> looking at camera, static pose, standing portrait, symmetrical limbs, cropped
> hair or feet, original dress/cardigan, leggings, shoes, sexualized framing,
> lingerie styling, duplicated tools or limbs, malformed fingers/toes,
> disconnected joints, impossible spine, Kaede identity bleed, camera-photo
> realism.
