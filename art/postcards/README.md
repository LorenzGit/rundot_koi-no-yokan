# Character Vacation Postcards

This folder contains collectible vacation-postcard illustrations for the
`KOI NO YOKAN` cast. Each postcard should preserve the referenced character's
identity, proportions, outfit, and established 1990s anime-cel art direction
while placing them in a playful tropical scene with a strong, characterful
action pose.

- Character reference: `public/images/cast/char_f_runner_figure.png`
- Reference rights: project-owned game artwork
- Generator: Codex built-in image generation

- Format: PNG, RGB, 1536 × 1024
- Status: style prototype pending approval

### Prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept
>
> Asset type: collectible vacation postcard illustration for the game KOI NO
> YOKAN
>
> Input images: Image 1 is the identity, costume, proportions, and
> illustration-style reference for Kaede. Preserve her exact recognizable
> face, high brown ponytail with teal tie, athletic body proportions, teal
> sleeveless running top, charcoal high-waisted leggings, teal running shoes,
> and black sports watch.
>
> Primary request: Create one polished landscape vacation postcard featuring
> Kaede in a fun tropical place doing something cute and highly dynamic.
>
> Scene/backdrop: a lush fictional tropical island lagoon with bright
> turquoise water, a small cel-painted waterfall, palms, hibiscus, sunlit
> volcanic rocks, and distant warm clouds. No other people.
>
> Subject/action: Show Kaede fully visible in an energetic rope-swing arc just
> above the lagoon. One hand grips the rope overhead; her free hand gives an
> enthusiastic wave toward an off-canvas friend. Her knees bend backward
> asymmetrically, one sneaker higher than the other, torso twists into the
> swing, ponytail streams dramatically behind her, and small water droplets
> sparkle below. She has a broad delighted vacation smile and a tiny hibiscus
> flower tucked behind one ear. The pose must feel airborne, playful, cute,
> and unmistakably dynamic—not a standing character pasted onto a background.
>
> Style/medium: match Image 1 closely: classic 1990s Japanese television-anime
> cel illustration, crisp thin dark ink, flat two-tone cel shading, expressive
> face, clean hand anatomy, saturated but cohesive tropical palette,
> hand-painted cel-background feeling. Not photorealistic, not 3D, not modern
> glossy digital rendering.
>
> Composition/framing: wide 3:2 landscape postcard composition. Kaede is the
> clear focal subject, large enough to read but fully visible from ponytail to
> shoe soles with generous safety margin. Use strong diagonal motion from
> upper-left rope to lower-right splash. Add a simple clean cream postcard
> border around the artwork, with slightly rounded printed-paper corners, but
> no writing.
>
> Lighting/mood: joyful late-afternoon tropical sunlight, warm and romantic,
> breezy and carefree.
>
> Constraints: preserve Kaede's identity and outfit; exactly one character;
> correct limbs and hands; full body visible; dynamic silhouette; coherent
> perspective; finished game-quality illustration.
>
> Avoid: text, letters, logos, stamps, postal marks, captions, watermark, extra
> characters, frontal static pose, standing pose, cropped feet, costume drift,
> duplicated limbs, malformed hands, camera-photo realism, gradients that
> overpower the cel style.
